    Slideshow hours

    A party still shows at any time. A location can have its own hours instead (Locations, Edit, Settings). Many TVs also have their own off timer in their settings, which saves power.

    Scheduled screen-off times

    On top of the slideshow hours: the screen goes off at these times for the minutes set, e.g. Saturday 10:00 for 5 minutes. A party still shows.
